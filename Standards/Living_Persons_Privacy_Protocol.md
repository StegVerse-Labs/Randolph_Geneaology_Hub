# Living Persons Privacy Protocol

1. No posting of:
   - Exact birthdates of living persons
   - Current addresses
   - Private contact details

2. Living individuals may be recorded with:
   - Canonical_ID
   - Birth year (if public)
   - Parentage linkage

3. No speculative children listings without public obituary confirmation.

4. All living entries default to Evidence_Level C until verified by:
   - Public obituary
   - Public military record
   - Published newspaper article
